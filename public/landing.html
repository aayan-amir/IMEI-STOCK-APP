Frequently asked questions

Can I use Stock-ly for more than one shop?

Yes. An account can have multiple configured shops, with warehouse receiving, dispatch records and separate shop access.

Does it work on a phone?

The web app works on desktop and mobile browsers. You can enter an IMEI manually or use a compatible camera or barcode scanner. Camera scanning needs browser permission.

How do I get an account or see a demo?

Contact sales at ayanwork1@gmail.com. Tell us how many shops you run and what you need to track, and ask for a walkthrough and pricing.

Can I take a copy of my records?

Owners can download business backups and export supported lists and reports. Ask us to walk you through what is included and how recovery works.

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Go to the dashboard and use your Client ID and site password. Your owner or shop access continues from there.
